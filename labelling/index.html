Listing labelling

Does the photo contradict this attribute value of the listing? Match: the photo is consistent with it. Mismatch: the photo clearly shows something different. Cannot tell: not visible, or the photo does not show the product. The title is context; the verdict is about the attribute value. You see no model output here.

Keys: 1 match, 2 mismatch, 3 cannot tell; Enter or right arrow next; left arrow back; up and down choose the attribute.
